Brand

Colour

POST's brand colours are POST Blue and white. Black is also used.

POST Blue is a highly saturated RGB colour, so print in Pantone Coated wherever possible. For the same reason, matching it in print and materials can take a bit of work.

Brand

The anchor. Brand blue is the primary; white / black are the base surface + text. The only color that lives in Foundations — Web and Marketing import these and build their own ramps.
