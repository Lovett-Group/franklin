Voice and tone

POST's voice tells stories in an authentic and direct tone — as if speaking to an old friend, at once erudite and accessible.

While our messaging is bold and fearless, it should also strive to be empathetic and judicious. Our stories radiate warmth, charm and humility.

Writing for POST

Do

  • Be concise and honest
  • Use plain English
  • Ask a question
  • Engage your audience
  • Use action verbs
  • Use words from our lexicon

Don't

  • Use hyperbole or exaggerate
  • Use clichés or marketing jargon
  • Overuse exclamation points
  • Engage in highly sensitive or potentially offensive discourse
  • Use criticism

Who is speaking

POST has three communication streams, and they are not the same voice.

The key distinction. We view ourselves as cheeky documentarians chronicling the activity at POST. A tenant's voice is focused on active promotion of their concept and on driving visitation.
POST speaking to guestsOur audience is the people who follow our social media, see our advertisements, view our website and visit our space. Campaigns should be authentic, engaging and reflective of that audience.
POST speaking to tenantsPOST is a resource that supports tenants with clear information about the project as well as guidelines for best media practice. Be informative, direct and to the point. Avoid marketing jargon and be transparent.
Tenants speaking to guestsTenants each have their own voice, and should leverage POST's existing assets in order to maximise brand reach and content engagement.
