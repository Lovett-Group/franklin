About POST

POST Houston is a landmark destination in the heart of downtown — 500,000 square feet of historic post office building, on a 16-acre campus, merging food, shopping, entertainment and the arts.

POST is more than a brand or a destination. It is an experience that encourages you to imagine what could be. Whether you are a foodie searching for local or international cuisine, an urban explorer seeking a new adventure, or an entrepreneur growing your company, there is something at POST that inspires the imagination.

As the most diverse city in the US, Houston is past due for a new cultural anchor that is at once accessible and boundary-pushing. This is a place where it is ok to get lost; a new adventure lies around the corner.

The name

The name POST bridges the building's history with its aspirations for innovative 21st century programming.

  • It references the post office while asking the question “What's next?”
  • It gives us a highly flexible logotype that can accommodate a range of possibilities.
The POST name is always stylised in uppercase, and is never joined with other words.

History

The building has been a gateway to Houston for almost a century. The brand is built on that history rather than around it.

WhenWhat happened
1934Built as the city's gateway for travellers and freight, serving as Houston's Grand Central Station.
late 1950sPurchased by the US Government and repurposed as the main post office for the city of Houston.
1962The post office opens.
1987Named for Barbara Jordan, the South's first female African American legislator elected to the US Congress.
2015The US Government decommissions the Barbara Jordan Post Office and later sells the building to Lovett Commercial.
todayPOST reinvigorates the city's Theatre District as an adaptive reuse cultural epicentre linking past and future.
